Writing for a non-native speaker can be difficult. While I’m very good at conversations and comprehension, writing in English feels unnatural to me. Writing and editing my thesis was one of the most challenging parts of my PhD. 



For the longest time, even when I could express my thoughts, grammar and spotting what sounded weird were difficult for me to recognize. Scientific writing sometimes came easily because I was supposed to follow certain structures, terminologies, and wording. But things did get lost in translation. Even with Grammarly double-checking everything, I felt like it was yet another restriction on writing regularly. 



So when AI came out, it felt like my writing was more organized. But soon it turned out that everything I saw as making my writing easier was something people hated because it sounded less human, which is counterintuitive to me because isn’t writing trained on human language? But I think it also gives me space now to be human, make mistakes, and not always worry about sentence structure.



 For my science + personal blog, I’d love your feedback on one thing: what do you prefer to read, and what makes you stop and read a technical but engaging article? Making science accessible to everyone is one reason I want to use my blog.
