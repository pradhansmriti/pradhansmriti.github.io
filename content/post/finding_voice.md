Writing for a non-native speaker can be difficult. While I think I'm actually very good at conversations and comprehension, writing in English feels very unnatural to me. Writing and editing my thesis was also one of the most challenging parts for me in my PhD. For the longest time, even if I can express my thoughts, grammar and making sure what sounds weird was difficult for me to recognize. Scientific writing came easy sometimes because there were certain structures, terminologies, wording I was supposed to follow. But things did get lost in translation. Even with Grammarly double-checking everything I wrote felt like yet another restriction to writing regularly. So when AI came out, it actually felt like my writing was more organized. But soon it turned out that all the things I saw as making my writing easy where things that people hated because it sound less human. Which is counter-intuitive to me because isn't writing trained from human language. But I think it also gives me space now to be human, make mistakes and not always worry about sentence structure? For a science+ personal blog what do you prefer? Making science accessible to everyone is one of the reasons I want to use my blog for. Would love some feedback on what writing you enjoy, what makes you stop a read a technical but engaging article?
