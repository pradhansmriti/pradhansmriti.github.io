Hi, I'm Smriti.

I'm a computational scientist who completed a Physics PhD in August 2026. I study how complex systems work by building physics based models that are computationally accessible and use machine learning methods and scientific computing to accelerate these simulations. Some complex systems I have worked in the past include virus assembly, behavior in ant colonies, hopfield networks etc.
